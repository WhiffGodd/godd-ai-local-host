Godd AI Privacy Policy

Connecting YouTube

Google handles sign-in and consent. Godd AI requests read access to identify and display the selected YouTube channel and upload access to send clips to that channel. The app uses the channel ID, channel name, and authorization tokens to provide those functions. It sends the rendered clip, title, description, visibility, audience setting, and thumbnail to YouTube when you choose Upload to YouTube.

Storage and access

The server keeps each studio's channel information and authorization tokens in memory. Connections expire after 24 hours or when the server restarts. Each studio uses a separate browser cookie to identify its connection. The current clip and its settings are kept in the browser tab during sign-in so the studio can reopen it when you return.

Source files you provide and rendered clips are stored temporarily for processing and exports. A clip uploaded to your YouTube channel is managed through YouTube Studio. Godd AI does not use Google account data for advertising or sell that data.

AI Video Studio uses the prompt you enter to make a storyboard and a temporary video. On a local Ollama setup the prompt is processed on your computer. If the server is configured to use Gemini, your prompt is sent to Google for storyboard generation.

Disconnecting your channel

Use Disconnect to remove the selected studio's session and its stored tokens. To revoke the full Google authorization, remove Godd AI from Google Account connections.

Google and YouTube policies

Godd AI uses YouTube API Services. Your use of those services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy explains how Google handles your information. Godd AI's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
